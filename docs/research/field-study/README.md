# Issue #11 — field-study execution kit

**Status: prepared for human review; NO participants recruited, interviews held, diaries collected, usability comparisons conducted, or product hypothesis validated.** This is a set of blank operating materials, not results.

**Research question:** Among adults who already use a personally trusted prayer-time source and ordinary planning tools, are there recurring, consequential **and solvable** coordination problems those tools do not handle adequately? If so, can a tiny reversible decision aid improve the outcome enough to justify maintaining another tool?

Read [Issue #11](https://github.com/Safeer1877/islamic-life-companion/issues/11), the [research MVP plan](../RESEARCH_MVP_VALIDATION_PLAN_v0.1.md), [research standards](../RESEARCH_STANDARDS.md), [privacy/mobile contract](../../architecture/MOBILE_RELIABILITY_PRIVACY_CONTRACT_v0.1.md) and [religious foundation](../../FOUNDATION_v1.md). The historical 106 state IDs and 19 candidate contracts are **not** implementation instructions. The app, participants, source/copy religious review, and physical-device tests do not yet exist.

## Working sequence

| Stage | Activity | Required output | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 — ethics and logistics | Designate real researcher/custodian, separate confidential reporting route, secure storage and retention, independent privacy/ethics assessment; determine applicable institutional/legal review | Completed, dated launch authorization in a **private** project record | **Do not recruit or collect** while incomplete |
| A — discovery | Invite a varied exploratory group (e.g. approximately eight adults in one accessible region through at least two unrelated routes, including people satisfied with current tools). Conduct one recent-episode interview per person; never pitch the app first | Participant-checked redacted episode maps, recruitment/refusal/missingness log | Stop/narrow if no recurring actionable unmet problem emerges |
| A2 — optional diary | Invite a *voluntary* short seven-day, low-pressure diary including no-conflict days; diary refusal does not disqualify interview findings | Coarse, participant-controlled observations and diary-burden note | Do not infer unrecorded days or religious observance |
| B — comparison **only if A warrants it** | Compare the participant's actual calendar + trusted prayer-information practice with paper/clickable **synthetic neutral** decision cards; counterbalance task order when feasible | Per-condition task and burden data, including no-action and impossible-case results | Do not proceed if prototype causes unacceptable pressure, authority confusion or errors |
| C — investment decision | Independently review de-identified evidence and negative cases; choose continue / narrow / pivot / postpone / stop | Public **aggregate-only** signed decision report with limitations | No broader implementation from unverified hypotheses |

The approximate interview count is an **exploratory batch size, not a statistical estimate or a universal “two of ten” threshold**. Predeclare and date the actual stopping/continuation rubric before participant work, and retain contrary cases. Adjust recruiting only through documented protocol amendments, never to manufacture a passing rate.

## Public/private separation

**Public GitHub:** protocol, empty templates, source citations, disclosed methodology, reviewed aggregate findings and redacted limitations **after disclosure review**.

**Never in this repository:** names, phone numbers, recruitment contact list, consent signatures, identifiable schedules, audio, screenshots, raw interviews/diaries, participant lookup keys, religious attendance, diagnoses, or correspondence with an identifiable participant. Participant IDs and coarse “anonymous-looking” schedules can still identify people; keep them private.

No raw research data in GitHub, model prompts, unapproved transcription tools, public forms, or shared drives without an actual documented privacy assessment. Using free tooling does not remove this requirement.

**Next human action:** appoint the researcher/data custodian, verify confidential contact and approved storage, arrange privacy/ethics review, then complete the pre-study decision rubric **before** distributing the invitation. Until then, Issue #11 remains open.

## Decision boundary

A successful study would support only a further **limited hypothesis test**, not a complete app, religious judgment, “universal Muslim” coverage, or claimed notification reliability. A result showing that existing tools or a paper worksheet work as well is valid evidence to narrow or stop the software proposal.
