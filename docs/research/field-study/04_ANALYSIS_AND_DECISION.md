# Qualitative coding, safety adjudication and research decision record

**Blank protocol and reporting template — no participant results exist yet.** Never fill example numbers as if measured. Completed individual coding and contact keys must remain in approved private storage.

## 1. Compact codebook (inductive additions allowed)

| Code | Meaning | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| NEED-A | Recurring *actionable* information/coordination gap that baseline fails to address adequately | A recent episode with actual feasible change, consequences and current workaround |
| BASELINE-WORKS | No new application needed for this task | A successful ordinary current-tools episode or explicit refusal of added burden |
| STRUCTURAL | Change requires employer/other-person permission, resources or a nonexistent opportunity | Identified constraint and who can actually authorize it |
| NO-CONFLICT | The person has no relevant problem in observation window | Explicit no-conflict response; do not infer from diary silence |
| SOURCE-UNKNOWN | Available information untrusted, stale or method unclear | Participant's actual uncertainty, not a guessed method |
| BURDEN | Entry, switching, correction, learning or social work required | Observed or participant-described effort, not an assumed number |
| AUTHORITY-RISK | Suggestion perceived as a religious or organizational permission | Participant teach-back or observed mistaken certainty |
| PRIVACY-REFUSAL | Data refused or exposure considered unacceptable | Voluntary refusal, without asking for content |
| HARM-OR-PRESSURE | Anxiety, unwanted interruption, coerced change, unsafe direction or distress | Reported/observed, handled privately |
| HYPOTHETICAL | No real episode/behavior observed; synthetic vignette only | Label explicitly; cannot prove real-world need |

Keep recalled events distinct from direct observation and inference. Multiple codes may apply. A recurring issue **without a feasible decision** is not evidence for an automated planner.

## 2. Coding and challenge process

1. Researcher A codes anonymized, consent-compatible **private** transcripts/episode summaries after removing identity-linked detail.
2. If available, a qualified second person independently codes the first two episodes, all serious adverse cases and a sample of the rest. Record disagreement and its resolution without inventing inter-rater scores.
3. If no second coder is available, mark the analysis **single-coded exploratory**, not independently validated.
4. Build a private case-by-theme matrix showing positive, negative and *disconfirming* episodes, including participants with adequate current tools and optional diary nonresponse.
5. Invite participant correction of **factual sequence**, not ratification of the founder's concept or researcher's interpretation.
6. Report participant denominators and methods honestly, including approached/consented/declined/completed, diary days offered/returned/missing, recruited-channel bias and contextual limitations. No population inference from convenience samples.

## 6. Allowed conclusions

- **CONTINUE narrow testing:** repeated, credible actionable unmet episodes survive a competent baseline; a low-fidelity task comparison subsequently shows useful gain without critical misinterpretation or unacceptable upkeep. No full-app release is authorized.
- **NARROW:** a particular task or group benefits, but generic planner/mosque features do not. Keep only the supported mechanism.
- **PIVOT:** the need is real but support, education, a better calendar setup or human coordination is more appropriate than software.
- **POSTPONE:** evidence promising but privacy custody, source rights, reviewers, devices, staffing or actual participants are unavailable or results inconclusive.
- **STOP the integrated-planner hypothesis:** repeated well-observed tasks reveal no actionable benefit over an adequate baseline, or the mechanism requires unacceptable surveillance, dubious authority, dangerous prompts or unsupportable maintenance.

A negative study does not disprove every possible future use, and a positive study does not justify inventing an adjacent feature to avoid a stop decision.
