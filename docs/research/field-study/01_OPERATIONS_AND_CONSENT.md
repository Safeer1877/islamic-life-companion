# Operational readiness, neutral recruitment and consent

**Template only — no study has been approved or carried out.** Complete this document in a **private** approved project workspace. Public copies must contain no contact details, signatures, personal data or identifiable study incidents.

## 1. Pre-recruitment authorization — all required

| Item | Record privately, then publicly mark only verified/unverified |
|---|---|
| Named researcher and independent reviewer | Real names/qualifications and declared conflicts held privately; never represent an AI as independent human oversight |
| Named data custodian and participant contact | Verified, safe confidential contact whose receipt has been tested |
| Location, language and initial inclusion | One reachable region; adults aged 18+; personal scheduling-tool use; no test of piety, prayer attendance, health or specific jurisprudential tradition |
| Consent and applicable oversight | Research/ethics reviewer approves exact materials; obtain institutional permission if a recruitment site requires it; assess applicable law |
| Storage and permissions | Access-controlled, encrypted workspace; contacts/consent separate from notes; named access list; backup and device loss plan |
| Retention, withdrawal and erasure | Dates, method, backup scope, consent/legal exceptions and limits recorded **before** participation |
| Incentive or expenses | Fixed, locally appropriate amount/conditions disclosed before agreement; not dependent on any answer or task success |
| Accessibility and risks | Accessible/Urdu/other-language version reviewed; interviewer training; session stops for distress, coercion, private third-party exposure |
| Protocol frozen | Version, date, recruitment criteria, method, stopping rule, independent reviewer acknowledgment |

**Blocker:** If any confidentiality, permission or participant-safety item is not ready, **do not recruit or solicit actual schedules**. An existing GitHub private vulnerability facility does not automatically constitute a confidential *research or conduct* inbox.

## 2. Neutral invitation — draft for review

> We are studying how adults coordinate fixed appointments, flexible personal activities and optional time information using the tools they already trust. We want to learn both what works well and what causes difficulty. We are **not** assessing religious practice or trying to persuade anyone to use a new app. Participation is voluntary and involves discussing a recent scheduling example, which you may anonymize or replace with a simplified account. You may skip any question or stop. We will explain privacy arrangements and any compensation privately before asking you to join. Please do not send your real calendar or personal details through a public GitHub issue.

Do not ask a mosque, university, employer or family member to nominate or disclose who participated. Independent circulation of an invitation is different from access to recruitment responses. Avoid recruiting only people who already support the founder.

## 3. Private screening questions — minimal and voluntary

- Are you an adult (18+), and able to consent independently? Do not record a birth date.
- Do you normally coordinate at least one fixed weekly commitment and some flexible personal time?
- Which broad tools do you use now (calendar, paper, reminders, a trusted source for time-related information, or none)? Do not ask for specific religious practice or documents.
- Which interview language and accessibility accommodations would you prefer?
- Are you willing to discuss a recent *anonymized* scheduling example? If not, you may decline; do not pressure an explanation.
- Are you connected with the founder/researcher, employer or study recruitment organization in a way that may affect freedom to refuse? Document the recruitment channel without public identification.

This first exploration may intentionally include people whose existing tools work well. Do not screen for “having a painful problem” and then report the artificially selected sample as prevalence.

## 4. Participant information and consent script — researcher must adapt and approve

**Purpose:** We are testing whether any new coordination tool is warranted, not validating a religious ruling or evaluating personal behavior. There is no functioning study app.

**Activity:** One optional interview (roughly 45–60 minutes). With *separate* consent, a voluntary short diary and/or later low-fidelity comparison may be offered; joining one does not oblige joining others. During tasks, use synthetic or participant-redacted entries; nothing changes the participant's real commitments.

**Privacy:** We avoid recording names of others, exact addresses, complete calendars, religious observance, medical/menstrual information and family conflict details. The participant controls whether to show a tool, and can instead describe a redacted sequence. Audio/screenshot/quote permission is **independent** of participation. No public raw data.

**Risks:** Sharing personal routines could reveal sensitive facts, and discussion may feel uncomfortable. The interviewer will not assess piety or provide religious/medical advice. Questions can be skipped.

**Withdrawal and deletion:** Explain a clear confidential contact, until-when identifiable records can be located/deleted, limits of truly de-identified published aggregates, and limits of backups or already-circulated outputs. Specify applicable legal retention exceptions. Do not promise universal deletion.

**Proposed retention for privacy review, not yet adopted:** Audio/screenshots (if separately allowed) deleted within 14 days of checked notes/transcription; contact links and identifiable notes within 30 days of the last interview; de-identified analysis working notes within 90 days after the decision. Adjust for actual law, consent, security arrangements and withdrawal promises *before recruitment*; record version.

**Separate opt-ins (default No):**
- [ ] I agree to the interview and understand voluntary participation.
- [ ] Optional note-taking during interview (explain actual type of notes).
- [ ] Optional audio recording.
- [ ] Optional screen or photo capture.
- [ ] Optional seven-day diary.
- [ ] Optional invitation for a later task comparison.
- [ ] Optional use of a checked, de-identified quotation in a public aggregate report.

The first item is required only if the person opts to participate. No other item is a prerequisite. Record actual consent privately; a checklist in a public repository does not record consent.

## 5. Researcher stop/incident rules

Stop or redirect when participants feel judged, coerced, unsafe, distressed, unsure whether advice is religiously authorized, or when someone else's personal information is exposed. Do not prompt for religious exemptions, symptoms, worship completion, passwords or secrets. Allow immediate skip, stop, redaction, or withdrawal without defending a choice.

Report an incident through the **verified private research channel**, without putting identifiable content in a GitHub issue. Only publish an aggregate, disclosure-reviewed description of a safety concern when safe and necessary.
