# Original AI-assisted desk-research reports — provenance and reuse

These five files were imported to preserve the project's early **AI-assisted desk research** and provide a traceable record of ideas considered before the [foundational specification](../../docs/FOUNDATION_v1.md).

They are **not** independent empirical research studies, academic peer review, a qualified Islamic scholarly endorsement, a validated religious reference library, or proof that the proposed app works.

## How to use the reports responsibly

1. Treat claims as **leads to verify**, not as accepted evidence.
2. Go to the original primary religious reference, technical vendor documentation or research paper and check what it actually supports.
3. Separate product decisions from technical feasibility, user-study evidence and qualified jurisprudential applicability.
4. Use independently checked findings in reviewed documents and cite them by specific source; do not silently rewrite the preserved original reports.
5. For redistribution or substantial reuse of a raw report, seek appropriate rights review rather than assuming it is licensed under the main documentation license.
