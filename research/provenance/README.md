# Archived research provenance index

This folder contains **project-created audit metadata**, not the five original AI-generated reports themselves.

- [URL inventory](original-report-url-inventory.csv) records each literal HTTP(S) URL from those archived reports with original filename and line number.
- [Source-rights triage](../../docs/research/RAW_REPORT_SOURCE_RIGHTS_TRIAGE_2026-10-24.md) explains what was checked, what was not, and why the imported reports are excluded from the project's blanket licenses.
- [Repeatable check](../../scripts/check_research_provenance.py) preserves the known Git blob hashes and checks the inventory stays aligned with original source bytes.

**Licensing:** The original editorial text and derived inventory in this directory are **CC BY-SA 4.0** to the extent the project has grantable rights in its original metadata. URLs and third-party names are identifiers; including them does not confer license rights in external websites or their contents. The five reports under `research/original-reports/` remain **excluded** and untouched. See [scoped license map](../../LICENSES/README.md).

**Meaning of PASS:** Unchanged archive and reproducible index only. No assertion that an external link works, a scientific conclusion is proven, religious guidance is approved or translation rights are cleared.
