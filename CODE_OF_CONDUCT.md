# Community code of conduct

Islamic Life Companion is intended to be a respectful, evidence-focused project that can be examined and improved by people with different backgrounds, skill levels and recognized religious perspectives.

## Expected conduct

- Treat people and religious traditions with dignity. Discuss claims, evidence, applicability and design choices rather than attacking contributors.
- Present differences in fiqh and interpretation accurately, without harassing or stereotyping people or groups.
- Respect those who ask for privacy, accessibility accommodations or corrections.
- Be open about limitations, conflicts of interest, financial relationships and substantive AI assistance.
- Acknowledge mistakes and improve them without shaming contributors.
- Do not share another person's private information or use repository discussions to solicit confidential religious, medical or family information.

## Unacceptable conduct

Personal attacks, threats, derogatory language, discriminatory harassment, sustained targeting, doxxing, spam, sexual harassment, deceptive impersonation of mosques/scholars, and attempts to pressure people into publicly declaring their religious practices or beliefs.

Do not treat contributors' disagreement as an invitation to judge their faith. The repository is a place to review source-backed research and software, not to adjudicate individuals' religious worth.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive public material where GitHub permissions permit, request changes, restrict participation, or refer serious abuse to GitHub.

For ordinary non-sensitive moderation concerns, open a GitHub issue describing the behavior **without reproducing private information**. For harassment or threats requiring platform action, use GitHub's **Report content or abuse** controls. Do not post sensitive details publicly.

**Open governance gap:** The maintainer has not yet published a confirmed private conduct-reporting inbox. Before actively growing a large community, a reliable confidential reporting route and enforcement owner must be configured; this document does not claim such a system currently exists.

## Scope

This standard applies to project issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions and affiliated community spaces operated by project maintainers. Participation remains subject to GitHub's own policies and terms.

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [review process](docs/governance/REVIEW_PROCESS.md).
