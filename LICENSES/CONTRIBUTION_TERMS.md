# Contribution licensing and provenance

**Established on repository owner approval:** 24 October 2026.  
**No assignment of copyright:** Contributors retain their rights to their own protectable work while granting recipients the rights established by the applicable project license.

## Inbound equals outbound

By deliberately submitting a contribution for inclusion in the project, a contributor must have authority to provide it under the outgoing license stated for that file's scope:

- **MPL-2.0** for original app, shared-client tools, scripts and other code assigned MPL in [the scope table](README.md).
- **AGPL-3.0-only** for original executable code in the designated hosted mosque/community service areas.
- **CC BY-SA 4.0** for original rights-controlled documentation and governance prose.
- **No general grant** over the separately excluded \`research/original-reports/\` or third-party materials: propose sources, citations and reviews separately; do not paste protected external work without authorization.

A contribution cannot transfer copyright the contributor does not possess. If an output is generated or substantively assisted by AI, state that clearly and assess embedded original third-party material and applicable provider terms; do not invent exclusive rights or scholarly endorsements.

## Proposed contribution attestation (for GitHub PR templates)

Contributors should include this plain-language declaration:

> I have the right to submit the material in this pull request under the applicable project license described in LICENSES/README.md. I have disclosed important AI assistance and third-party source materials, and I have not intentionally included credentials, personal data or restricted third-party content. I understand that I retain my applicable copyright and am not transferring it to the maintainer.

**No DCO sign-off or CLA is required at this stage.** The project may later propose a DCO or CLA with transparent governance and contributor notice, but none is activated solely by this document.

For changes to Islamic rulings or fiqh-sensitive UI text, licensing permission and a citation are not a substitute for qualified scholarly review. See governance processes once merged; no model can grant such scholarly authority.

## Review gate

Before merge, a maintainer should verify the file's chosen license scope, the submitter's stated rights/provenance, and any compatibility or legal red flags. If uncertain, request a narrower patch, attributed link or qualified assessment rather than claiming rights were cleared.
